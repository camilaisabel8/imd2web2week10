Flamingos are famous for their bright pink feathers, long legs, and graceful curved necks. Their color comes from pigments in the algae and tiny crustaceans they eat.

Chameleons are remarkable lizards known for their independently moving eyes, long projectile tongues, and curled tails. Many can change their skin color to communicate, regulate their body temperature, or blend into their surroundings. They use their grasping feet to move carefully through branches in search of insects.
